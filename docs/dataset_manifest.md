# Dataset manifest

- Dataset status: **exploratory binary training completed; not adequate for reliable performance claims**.
- Curation date: 2026-10-25 (Asia/Calcutta).
- Task: protein-level research classification for human ischemic-stroke association; this is not a person-level diagnosis or stroke-risk prediction.
- Label rule: see `docs/evidence_and_labels.md`. A positive requires a primary human ischemic-stroke study and a documented risk-before-event, post-event-measurement, or severity/outcome relationship. These categories remain distinct.
- Current evidence curation: 68 rows: 56 positive, 10 provisional candidate_negative, 2 unresolved (F8 and CST5). Unresolved cases stay in curation and are excluded from training. Candidate-negative is absence of qualifying evidence in a limited documented search, not a biological negative.
- Sequence set: 66 human RefSeq sequence records and FASTA entries (56 positive, 10 candidate_negative). Retrieved from NCBI Protein E-utilities on 2026-10-25. Records were checked for accession, Homo sapiens, nonempty sequence, standard amino-acid alphabet, duplicate accession, and duplicate sequence. Source URLs, gene IDs, retrieval date, accession uncertainty, and sequence lengths are in `data/processed/proteins_esm.csv`.
- Additional source reviewed for candidate generation: Chen et al., “Targeted plasma proteomics reveals a central role of upregulated TNFRSF proteins in HIV-associated stroke,” Nature Communications (2026), PMID 42265127, DOI 10.1038/s41467-026-74258-8. It is a primary human proteomics study; 184 proteins were quantified and its ischemic-stroke supplement categorizes 27 proteins as Up/Down and 155 as Ns. The cohort is people living with HIV, limiting generalizability. The 155 Ns entries are only candidate leads, not negative labels. Source workbooks are retained in `data/raw/`; the 155-entry alias-aware PubMed search pool is `data/raw/2026_ischemic_candidate_search_pool.csv`.
- PubMed screen status for the 155-entry pool: alias-aware E-utilities searches completed and were checkpointed in `data/raw/2026_ischemic_pubmed_search_results.csv`; a synonym-expanded follow-up covered initial zero-hit candidates (`data/raw/2026_ischemic_pubmed_expanded_zero_results.csv`). Seven proteins remained zero-hit after expansion and were assigned provisional candidate_negative labels: CPA1, CTRC, SCGB3A2, HAO1, IL17D, PRSS27, and PIGR. These searches are not exhaustive and zero hits do not prove biological absence.
- From the Lagging 2022 91-protein human study (PMID 36418382; DOI 10.1038/s41598-022-23288-5), three residual candidates were searched and manually reviewed: KYNU, CSF2RA, and FCRL2. No qualifying protein-specific human ischemic-stroke evidence was found in the limited review; they are provisional candidate negatives. This review does not convert the panel's nonsignificant results into negatives. Source query logs are retained in `data/raw/2022_91panel_pubmed_results.csv`.
- Previous targeted PubMed/PMC review supports the 48 positive rows. Search scope is not systematic. GEO and database annotations were not used as proof of a positive.
- Eight additional positive decisions were added after primary-human review: ALCAM, CD84, LGALS4, PRTN3, BLMH, CCL15, IL16, and AGRP. The AGRP report is a preprint (PMID 40832370) and is identified as such in the curation table. The new evidence includes small, selected, and subgroup-specific studies; see `docs/evidence_and_labels.md` and the row-level table.
- Similarity groups: pairwise local alignment, identity >=30% and aligned coverage >=80% for both sequences, connected components. 66 sequences form 61 groups: positive 51, candidate_negative 10. This portable fallback was used because bundled MMseqs2 failed on Windows.
- Training completed with frozen `esm2_t6_8M_UR50D` layer 6 mean-pooled embeddings and L2 logistic regression. Seed-42 grouped split: train 46 (40/6 rows; 36/6 independent groups), validation 9 (7/2 rows; 7/2 groups), test 11 (9/2 rows; 8/2 groups), where pairs are positive/candidate_negative. Threshold 0.05 was selected on validation F1. Test precision 0.80, recall 0.89, F1 0.84, balanced accuracy 0.44, PR-AUC 0.72, ROC-AUC 0.17, confusion matrix `[[0,2],[1,8]]`. Both test candidate-negatives were missed. Metrics are saved in ignored `results/model3_esm2/metrics.json` and are extremely uncertain; this run is suitable only as a software demonstration, not evidence of useful biological classification.
- Limitations: targeted non-systematic evidence review, heterogeneous populations and assays, provisional absence-of-evidence labels, only two test candidate-negative proteins, severe uncertainty, HIV-specific discovery pool, selected and exploratory subgroup findings, one chosen RefSeq isoform per gene, no clinical or structure features. Association is not causation and predictions do not concern a person's stroke.
- Reproduce: install requirements, run `python scripts/check_dataset_readiness.py`, then `python scripts/run_model3_esm2.py --data data/processed/proteins_esm.csv --output results/model3_esm2`. ESM-2 weights download on first run and remain in ignored local cache. Start the demo using `.venv\Scripts\python.exe app.py`.
