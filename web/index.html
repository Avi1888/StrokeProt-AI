Model and held-out results

A frozen ESM-2 encoder converts the sequence into one mean-pooled vector. Regularized logistic regression maps that vector to a score. Related sequences were kept in the same train, validation, or test split. The encoder was not fine-tuned.

The dataset is small, class-imbalanced, and targeted rather than systematic. Candidate negatives mean “no qualifying evidence found under the logged search,” not biological negatives. The held-out test scores are too uncertain to establish useful performance.

For research demonstration only. A documented protein association is not proof of causation.
